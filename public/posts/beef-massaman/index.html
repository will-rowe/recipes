Beef massaman

Sweet and spicy, slow cooked beef in a coconut based sauce

  • Thai
  • Beef
  • Curry
added 25 December 2020 by Will

YIELDS

4 bowls

PREP TIME

30 minutes

COOK TIME

6 hours

Ingredients -

curry

  • 250 grams

    beef shin

  • 1

    carrot

  • 50 grams

    cashew nuts

  • 1 stick

    cinnamon

  • 100 millilitres

    coconut milk

  • 2

    kaffir lime leaves

  • 4

    medium potatoes

  • 4

    red chillies

  • 4 pieces

    star anise

paste

  • 2

    birdseye chillies

  • 5 pods

    cardamom

  • 4 cloves

    garlic

  • 10 grams

    ginger

  • 10 grams

    lemongrass

  • 20 grams

    peanuts

  • 1

    shallot

  • 1 tsp

    shrimp paste

  • 1 tbsp

    tomato puree

Adjust number of bowls:

Method -

  • Use a pestle and mortar to pound the paste ingredients and form the paste.

  • Cube the beef and brown in some peanut oil in a cast iron pot, then remove.

  • Add some more peanut oil to the pan, bring up the heat and add the coconut milk.

  • Add the paste and stir through, cooking until fragrant.

  • Dice the potatoes and carrots, add to the pot along with the beef and remaining curry ingredients.

  • Cover the pan and cook in an oven for 5 hours at 110°C.

  • Serve over rice with lime, coriander and some green beans.
